Teaching Assistant

I was a teaching assistant in the Department of Mathematics, University of Crete, from 2005 to 2014 (officially) and from 2014 to 2017 (unofficially, as a volunteer to accommodate department needs). Among the courses I have taught to graduate or undergraduate students are: Algebra, Number Theory, Logic, Probabilities, Analysis and Design of Algorithms, Discrete Mathematics, Numerical Analysis, Numerical Solution of ODEs, Cryptology, Linear Algebra, Analytic Geometry, Group Theory, Galois Theory, Analysis and Foundation of Mathematics, Decidability.

I also helped students with programming in various languages and frameworks such as Python, C/C++, Maple, Matlab, LaTeX/XeTeX/BibTeX and Ipe (graphics editor). Finally, I trained undergraduate students for the GRE-general and GRE-mathematics tests.
